# Hook Framework — First 3 Seconds

> Reference for short-form video (Instagram Reels / TikTok). The first 3 seconds determine whether someone watches or swipes. There are three separate layers — you need all three.

---

## The Three Layers

### 1. Visual Hook (0–1 sec) — What they SEE before they process words
The first frame is seen before the sound. This decides the pause.
- Motion in frame beats static every time
- Face + activity beats just face
- Text overlay on the first frame (as caption / hook text) dramatically improves stop rate

**Rule:** The first frame should tell them WHAT WORLD they're entering, not who you are.

### 2. Audio Hook (1–3 sec) — The first line
Must create an open loop — something unresolved that their brain wants closed.

Types:
| Hook Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Confession | "I was doing X wrong for Y" | "I ran 3 restaurants for two years blind." |
| Number | Lead with a specific surprising number | "Rp 130 juta a month. One kitchen. 5 staff." |
| Contrast | Unexpected juxtaposition | "This looks like a warung. It's doing 4× the margin." |
| Pain | Name their exact problem | "If you're still managing everything in your head..." |
| Curiosity gap | Show the answer, withhold the path | "I asked an AI to run my morning briefing. Here's what happened." |
| Pattern interrupt | Drop in mid-scene, no intro | Start mid-action, mid-sentence. Never "hey guys." |

**Rule:** Never start with your name, "hey guys," or "today I want to talk about." Open loops only.

### 3. Curiosity Hook (the reason they don't leave at 3 sec)
After the first line, they need ONE thing that makes them think: *I want to know how this ends.*
- A visible problem without the solution yet
- A claim that sounds impossible
- A process they've never seen before

---

## The "Action While Talking" Principle

**Why it works:**
- The brain tracks moving objects involuntarily — motion = attention
- Contextual action signals expertise without claiming it (you're IN the kitchen = you know kitchens)
- Dual-channel engagement: watching the action + processing the audio simultaneously = higher retention
- Feels natural, not staged — which is the aesthetic of high-performing organic content in 2025–2026

**Key rule:** The action must be RELATED to the topic. Random action = distraction. Contextual action = authority.

### For Listy's content — action-topic mapping:

| Topic | Action to film while talking |
|---|---|
| Restaurant operations | Walking LalaFun floor, checking something on phone, reviewing printed reports |
| Numbers / cash flow | Fingers on a calculator, scanning a dashboard on laptop |
| Food / menu R&D | Cutting, tasting, checking texture (like pedesan) |
| System / AI build | Typing in terminal, looking at code on screen, side-by-side with Claude chat |
| Management decisions | Writing on whiteboard, flipping through notebook |
| Hiring / staffing | Walking past kitchen line, pointing at staff positions |

**Camera framing for action hooks:**
- Close crop on hands doing the action — don't show face yet, let them wait for it
- OR over-the-shoulder on what you're looking at (screen, notebook, pan)
- Cut to face at the hook line payoff (usually word 10–15)

---

## What Kills a Hook

- Starting with 2+ seconds of silence / music with no visual movement
- "Before I start, make sure to follow me"
- Text on screen that's too small to read as thumbnail
- Starting with your face doing nothing
- Explaining WHAT the video is about instead of starting the story

## Formula for This Series (Restaurant System Journey)

**The series premise:** One operator, 3 restaurants, no visibility → built an AI system from scratch.

**Episode arc structure:**
- Ep 1: The problem hook — "I had 3 restaurants and zero visibility"
- Ep 2: The moment I decided to build — what broke the camel's back
- Ep 3: What I built (show the actual system working)
- Ep 4: First result — what changed after week 1
- Ep 5+: Ongoing — decisions I made because the system showed me something

**Hook anchor line for the series:**
> "I run three restaurants. I'm building the brain that connects all of them. This is week [N]."

This creates a running context that makes returning viewers feel like they're watching a live documentary.

## Quick Reference — First Line Templates

Pick one, make it yours:

1. `"I run [N] [thing]. I had no idea what was happening in any of them."`
2. `"[Specific number] — that's what this [thing] does. Here's how."`
3. `"Most [role] don't track this. I track it every day."`
4. `"I made a [Rp X] mistake because I didn't have [system]. Here's what I built."`
5. `"This is what [Y] looks like when you actually build the infrastructure first."`
